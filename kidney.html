About Kidney

Vital Organ Responsible for Blood Circulation

Kidney - Anatomy and Function

General Overview:

Kidneys are a pair of bean-shaped organs located on either side of the vertebral column in the posterior abdominal cavity.

Function:

  • Filter blood
  • Form urine
  • Maintain fluid and electrolyte balance

Kidney Functions:

Filtration of blood

Regulation of:

  • Blood pressure
  • pH
  • Electrolyte balance

Hormone production:

  • Erythropoietin
  • Renin

External Features of the Kidney:

    1. Shape: Bean-shaped
    2. Size: ~10–12 cm long, 5–6 cm wide, 3 cm thick
    3. Weight: 120–170 grams each
    4. Surfaces:

  • Anterior surface
  • Posterior surface
    • 5. Borders:
  • Lateral border – convex
  • Medial border – concave (contains the hilum)
    • 6. Poles:
  • Superior pole (near adrenal gland)
  • Inferior pole

  • Positioned between T12 – L3 vertebrae
  • Right kidney is slightly lower than the left due to the liver.

Blood Supply:

Renal Artery → Segmental → Interlobar → Arcuate → Interlobular
Renal Vein → IVC

Internal Structure:

Renal Capsule → Cortex → Medulla → Minor & Major Calyces

Nerve Supply:

From renal plexus. Sympathetic nerves regulate blood flow.
